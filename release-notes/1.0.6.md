## What's new in 1.0.6

- **A "Sold!" moment at the till.** When a sale goes through, a coin drops into the tick on the receipt card and "Sold!" (Arabic "انباعت!") pops above the title, over in about half a second. It is on screen only: the printed receipt is unchanged.
- **A livelier QR menu.** Dishes come in one after another (capped, so a long menu never waits), lift under a mouse and give under a finger, the chosen category pops, the empty plate wiggles and the loader says "Setting the table…". With reduced motion turned on, everything shows at once and nothing moves.
- **The QR menu in Arabic and English.** It opens in the language of the guest's phone, and a button in the header switches it and remembers the choice. Arabic reads right to left with prices kept left to right. Dish and category names stay as typed at the till.

Nothing changes in your data: the app id, the PIN rules and the `%APPDATA%\EA POS` data folder are the same, so installing over 1.0.5 keeps every product, sale and setting.

### Installing

This build is unsigned, so installed tills do not update by themselves. Download `Zirr-POS-Setup-1.0.6.exe`, check its SHA-256 below, and run it on each till. Windows SmartScreen will warn about an unknown publisher: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
